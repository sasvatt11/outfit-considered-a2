How this works

Photo: kept in this browser. Garments: suggested by a free local vision model when requested, then reviewed by you. Colours: sampled from pixels and confirmed by you. Score and swap: calculated by inspectable rules. Occasion has priority; season is a small optional palette cue. A skin-area sample affects the colour-near-face component only after you pick it. Your pre-score view does not affect the score. No gender is inferred.
